A **Cross-Border E-Service** is a digital service that enables communication and data exchange between organizations across two different **Knodes**. Its purpose is to make the data and digital functionalities of one organization (the **Producer**) securely, standardly, and controllably available to another organization (the **Consumer**).

**Cross-Border E-Services** represent the core of the **Knodia** interoperability model: each organization can either produce or consume digital services from other organizations, ensuring traceability, security, and centralized permission management.
