*Producers* associate to *E-Services* the metadata useful to present it to *Operators* of other *Confederate Organizations*. These include, by way of non-exhaustive example, the name, description, and any additional documentation. The metadata must be provided at least in the common language accepted by the *Cross-Border Federation*.

*Interop Nodes* must ensure the following requirement is met:

- Publised *E-Services* include the metadata required by the *Cross-Border Federation* at least in its accepted common language.
