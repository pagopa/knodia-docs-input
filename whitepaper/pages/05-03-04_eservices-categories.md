# E-Service Federated Categories

*Interop Nodes* must allow *Producers* to associate E-Services to categories that are shared within the *Cross-Border Federation*. This is intended to simplify searching for services, using categories that are standardized across all Interoperability Domains.

By way of example, consider the need to identify the *Cross-Border E-Services* that provide data ona citizen's main address of residence. Several *Interop Nodes* will include E-Services that provide this data, and *Interop Nodes* can translate this concept as relevant in each Interoperability Domain: "residenza" for Italy, "Wohnsitz" for Germany, etc.

The *Cross-Border Federation* must therefore define and share a controlled vocabulary for Federated Categories. In this example, this controlled vocabulary will contain the category "main address" to identify this concept. Each *Interop Node* can render the *Federated Attributes* translated into the languages of its Interoperability Domain.

The lifecycle of this controlled vocabulary is a responsibility of the Federation Consortium, as reported in § [Considerations on the governance of the Cross-Border Federation](./05-11-00_federation-governance.md).

*Interop Nodes* must ensure the following requirement is met:

- E-Services can be associated to Federated Categories and are preferably associated to at least one Federated Category.
