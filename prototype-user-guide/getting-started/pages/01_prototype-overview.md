## What it is

The **Knodia prototype** is the infrastructure used to test **secure and standardized data exchange** between two **Knodes**. Stakeholders can register, publish their electronic services, and access those of other parties through uniform authentication, authorization, and tracking processes.

The **Knodia prototype** was created by **PagoPA**: 
- Demonstrate the applicability of the cross-border federated interoperability model defined in the [**Knodia whitepaper**](../../../whitepaper/pages/01_abstract.md).
- Implement a significant portion of the functionalities envisioned in the defined model.

The **Knodia prototype** implements two Knodes, **Orion Knode** and **Lynx Knode**, which are made available to stakeholders to test this cross-border federated interoperability model.

## Knodia prototype objectives

The **Knodia prototype** aims to demonstrate:

- **Simplification** of administrative procedures, resulting in reduced time and costs.
- **Interoperability** between parties, thanks to shared technical and operational rules.
- **Governance and transparency** through the tracking and control of administrative operations.

## The "only once" principle

The **Knodia prototype** enables testing of the **only once** principle in a cross-border context. Public administrations do not require citizens and businesses to provide information already held by other public administrations—even those from other countries—as they can retrieve it through the electronic services of the entities that hold the data and are registered in a Knode.
