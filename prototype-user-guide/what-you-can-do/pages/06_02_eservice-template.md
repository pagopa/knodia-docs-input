A **Producer** can define an **Cross-Border E-Services** using templates made available on their **Knode**.

**E-Service Templates** simplify the **Producer**'s tasks by defining the technical characteristics of **Cross-Border E-Services**.

In **Knodia**, **E-Service Templates** are designed to facilitate co-design processes between participating organizations.

The **Knodia Prototype** includes preconfigured **E-Service Templates** for illustrative purposes; users can instantiate them but not define new **E-Service Templates**.

For more details on the **E-Service Templates** additional feature, see [E-Service Templates](06_02_eservice-template.md).
