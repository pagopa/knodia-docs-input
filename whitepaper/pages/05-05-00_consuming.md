This section describes the functionality that *Interop Nodes* must provide to their *Confederate Organizations* concerning *Agreement Requests* and *Purpose Statements* that allow *Consumers* in the *Cross-Border Federation* to consume *E-Services*.

As a whole, the actions described below and in the information that is *Electronically Archived* by the interested parties digitally constitute an interoperability agreement between *Producer* and *Consumer*, in the meaning indicated by the *EIF*.

The section also describes the difference between the two modes of data transmission for *E-Services*:

- *Provide-Data*, in the case where the *Producer* sends data to the *Consumer*;
- *Receive-Data*, in the case where the *Producer* receives data from the *Consumer*.
