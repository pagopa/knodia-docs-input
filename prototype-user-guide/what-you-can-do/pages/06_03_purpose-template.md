A **Consumer** can define a **Purpose Statement** using templates made available on its **Knode**.

**Purpose Statement Templates** simplify the **Consumer**'s tasks by pre-populating **Purpose Statements** for specific administrative and technical scenarios.

The **Knodia Prototype** includes preconfigured **Purpose Statement Templates** for illustrative purposes; users can use them but cannot define new **Purpose Statement Templates**.

For more details on the **Purpose Statement Templates** additional feature, see [Purpose Statement Templates](06_03_purpose-template.md).
