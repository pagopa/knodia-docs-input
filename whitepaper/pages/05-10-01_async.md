The consumption described up to now occurs synchronously: the *Cross-Border E-Service* responds directly to the *Consumer*’s request with the requested data. An *Interop Node* must provide also an alternative mode that *Producers* can use to respond asyncronously.

A *Producer* that intends to use the asynchronous mode for a *Cross-Border E-Service* must declare the characteristics of the callback endpoint that the *Consumer* must implement to receive the data.

When calling the *Cross-Border E-Service*, the *Consumer* indicates the endpoint of the callback. When the *Producer* completes the processing of the reques and produces the resources to return, it calls the callback endpoint indicated by the *Consumer*.

The Federation Consortium referred to in § [Considerations on the governance of the Cross-Border Federation](./05-11-00_federation-governance.md), capitalizing on the effects of the proposed experimentation, defines in detail how to implement this functionality.
