**Knodes** allow operators of an onboarded organization, through the **Knode Catalogue**, to consult and manage **Cross-Border E-Services**.

As a **Consumer**, you can:
- Search for **Cross-Border E-Services** published by **Producers** from other **Knodes**.
- View the information sheets for the **Cross-Border E-Services** of interest.
- Consult and manage **Cross-Border E-Services** for which a request has been made, regardless of the status of that request (e.g., verifying the acceptance of an **Agreement Request**).

As a **Producer**, you can:
- Consult and manage defined **Cross-Border E-Services**, regardless of their status (e.g., suspending an active **Cross-Border E-Service**).
