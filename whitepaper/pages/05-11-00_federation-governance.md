# Considerations on the governance of the Cross-Border Federation

As mentioned at the beginning, a Federation Consortium – including the Managers of the *Interop Nodes* – will need to exist. This Consortium will define and update the technical elements needed for the *Cross-Border Federation*, capitalizing on the effects of the proposed experimentation.

The main task of this Consortium is to define the *Federated API* to be implemented by *Interop Nodes* and to manage the semantic objects used by *Interop Nodes*.

The Federation Consortium will be responsible to define and evolve the shared artifacts necessary to enable cross-border interactions. Specifically, these include:

- the controlled vocabularies of *Federated Attributes* and E-Service Federated Categories (as defined in the corresponding sections);
- the specification of the *Federated API* which every *Interop Node* must provide to other *Interop Nodes* to retrieve data and events for the various resources (this should be based on the needs identified in § [Distribution of knowledge between Interop Nodes](./05-11-01_distribution-of-knowledge.md));
- the set of events that *Interop Nodes* need to track for each resource and to publish using dedicated endpoints of the *Federated API*, so other *Interop Nodes* can react to them;
- the specification of the *Interop Node* API that every *Interop Node* must provide to Organizations to retrieve and manage resources programmatically;
- the definition of the format of the *Purpose Statement* forms exchanged between *Interop Nodes*;
- the details of the additional functionality proposed in § [Additional functionality provided by Interop Nodes](./05-10-00_additional-functionality.md);
- any vocabulary, ontology, specification, or data schema for which the need emerges during the prototyping and evolution of the *Cross-Border Federation*.
