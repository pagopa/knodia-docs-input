The **Consumption Keychain** is created by the **Consumer** on the **Knode** where it is onboarded.

A **Consumption Keychain** is associated with the operators who can manage it and, specifically, stores the public keys used for authentication.

The public keys for the **Knodia Prototype** are [JWK](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc7517) [RS256](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc7518) compliant and stored in PEM format.

The **Consumer** associates the purposes relating to a **Cross-Border E-Service** with the keychain containing the public keys used by the clients that will access that **Cross-Border E-Service**.
