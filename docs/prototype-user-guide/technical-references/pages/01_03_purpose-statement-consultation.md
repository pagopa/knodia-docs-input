**Knodes** allow operators of an onboarded party, through the **Knode Catalogue**, to consult and manage **Purpose Statement**.

As a **Consumer**, you can:
- view the **Purpose Statement** for received **Agreement Request** you have made, regardless of their status;
- suspend a previously created **Purpose Statement**.

As a **Producer**, you can:
- view the **Purpose Statement** declared for received **Agreement Request**, regardless of their status;
- suspend an active **Purpose Statement**;
- retrieve the **Consumer** contact information for a **Purpose Statement**.
