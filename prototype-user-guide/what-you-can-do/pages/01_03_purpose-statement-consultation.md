**Knodes** allow operators of an onboarded organization, through the **Knode Catalogue**, to consult and manage **Purpose Statements**.

As a **Consumer**, you can:
- View the **Purpose Statements** for the **Agreement Requests** you have made, regardless of their status.
- Suspend a previously created **Purpose Statement**.

As a **Producer**, you can:
- View the **Purpose Statements** declared for received **Agreement Requests**, regardless of their status.
- Suspend an active **Purpose Statement**.
- Retrieve the **Consumer's** contact information for a **Purpose Statement**.
