Within the *Cross-Border Federation*, *cross-border interactions* to share data and/or integrate services are realized through *Cross-Border E-Services*.

Organizations make *Cross-Border E-Services* available – hereinafter *produce* them – to allow other interested organizations to use them – hereinafter *consume* them – by defining an application programming interface (in short, API) and the associated metadata that characterize it.

The *Cross-Border Federation* provides for the existence of the following actors:

- the *Interoperability Nodes* (hereinafter *Interop Nodes*), which are the managers responsible for identifying and managing each a distinct set of *Confederate Organizations*, offering them the functionality needed to enable interaction with *Confederate Organizations* of other *Interop Nodes*;
- the *Confederate Organizations*, which are the organizations that assume either or both roles of:
  - *Producer*, when they provide *E-Services* to other *Confederate Organizations*;
  - *Consumers*, when they consume the *Cross-Border E-Services* of the *Producers*.

Taken together, the *Interop Nodes*, *Producers*, and *Consumers* constitute the *Cross-Border Federation*, which is based on trust established between the participating *Interop Nodes*. *Producers* and *Consumers* are recognized as participants in the *Cross-Border Federation* and carry out *cross-border interactions* between them, based on the information exchanged by the *Interop Nodes* that have recognized them.

A consortium of the *Interop Node* managers (hereinafter *Federation Consortium*) is necessary to establish the governance of the *Cross-Border Federation*. The appropriate venues shall evaluate whether this consortium can be implemented by the *Interoperable Europe Board* defined in Article 15 of the *Interoperable Europe Act*.

The *Interop Nodes* equip themselves with the technological components needed to join the *Cross-Border Federation*, following the requirements defined and shared by the *Federation* itself. *Producers* and *Consumers* adopt the communication standard and protocols identified by the *Cross-Border Federation* to carry out *cross-border interactions* between themselves and machine-to-machine interactions with the *Interop Nodes*.

In the following, where not ambiguous, the terms *Interop Nodes*, *Producers*, and *Consumers* are used to indicate both the organizations and the technological components they implement to join the *Cross-Border Federation*.
