**Keychains** are the tool made available to organizations that have completed onboarding on a **Knode** to store public cryptographic material.

**Knodes** ensure access to this material for those who need it, primarily to other **Knodes** for organization identification purposes.

The **Knodia Prototype** manages three types of **Keychains**:
- **Consumption**: To store the public keys used by clients to identify themselves when accessing a Producer's **Cross-Border E-Service**.
- **Production**: To store the public keys used to sign the responses of the organization's own **Cross-Border E-Service**.
- **Knode API**: To store the public keys used by clients to identify themselves when accessing the APIs made available by the **Knode** where the organization is onboarded.
