**Knodes** in **Knodia Prototype** implemented, as an example for users, the additional functionality envisaged [**Knodia whitepaper**](../../../whitepaper/pages/01_abstract.md).

Specifically:
- **Distribution of data change signals (Signal Hub)**, to allow users to experiment with the possibility of distributing entity change signals;
- **Producer Keychains**, users can create and associate them with their **Cross-Border E-Services** to sign their API responses;
- **E-Service Templates**, templates available to users for defining their **Cross-Border E-Services**;
- **Purpose Statement Templates**, templates available to users for compiling **Purpose Statements**;
- **Asynchronous communication**, a **Cross-Border E-Services** mockup has been made available for users to use, implementing the MEP (Message Exchange Pattern) Asynchronous Request-Reply with Polling.
