This section describes how *Knodes* issue *access tokens* for the *Cross-Border E-Services* of their *Producers* to the *Consumers* of other *Knodes* in *Knodia*. *Access tokens* are issued only if there exists an active *Agreement Request* and at least one active *Purpose Statement* for that *Consumer*.

The type of *access token* required to access a specific *Cross-Border E-Service* is defined by the *Producer* when creating the *Cross-Border E-Service*. The *Consumer* requests a *access tokens* consistently with the *Producer*’s decision.
