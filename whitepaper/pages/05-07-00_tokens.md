The following paragraph describes how *Producer Interop Node* issue *access tokens* for the *Cross-Border E-Services* of their *Producers* to the *Consumers* of other Interop Node in the *Cross-Border Federation*. *Access tokens* are issue only if there exists an *Agreement Request* and at least one *Purpose Statement* for that *Consumer*.

The type of *access token* required to access a specific E-Service is defined by the *Producer* when creating the *Cross-Border E-Service*. The *Consumer* requests an *access token* consistently with the *Producer*'s decision.
