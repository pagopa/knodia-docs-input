## What it is

The **Knodia prototype** is the infrastructure that allows testing of **secure and standardized data exchange** across two **Knodes**. Stakeholders register, publish their electronic services, and access those of other parties through uniform authentication, authorization, and tracking processes.

The **Knodia prototype** was created by **PagoPA** to demonstrate the applicability of the cross-border federated interoperability model defined in the [**Knodia whitepaper**](../../../whitepaper/pages/01_abstract.md).

The **Knodia prototype** implements two Knodels, **Orion Knodel** and **Lynx Knode**, made available to stakeholders to test the cross-border federated interoperability model.

## Objectives

* **Simplification** of administrative procedures, resulting in reduced time and costs.

* **Interoperability** between parties, thanks to shared technical and operational rules.

* **Governance and transparency**, through the tracking and control of administrative operations.

## The "only once" principle

The **Knodia prototype** enables the implementation of the "only once" principle in a cross-border context. Public administrations do not require citizens and businesses to provide information already held by other public administrations, even those from other countries, as they can obtain it through the electronic services of the entities that hold the data and register in a Knode.

## Benefits

**For institutions, organizations, and businesses:** standardized access to information, uniform authorization processes, reuse of assessments, and greater organizational efficiency.

**For citizens:** **faster procedures**, fewer repetitive formalities, and more uniform digital services nationwide.

## Data Management

The data for which **Producers** are responsible, the stakeholders who provide **Cross-Border E-Services**, **does not transit** and **is not stored** in **Knodes**.
