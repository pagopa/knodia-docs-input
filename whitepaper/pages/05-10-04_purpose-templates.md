*Knodes* may also provides *Purpose Statement* Templates that Organization can use to have a pre-filled version of the *Purpose Statement*, including any additional information that is requested to *Consumers* in accordance with the national regulatory framework.

A *Consumer* with an active *Agreement Request* for a *Cross-Border E-Service* can view the E-Service’s *Purpose Statement* Templates, if there are any, and choose whether to use them when creating a *Purpose Statement*.

Using a *Purpose Statement* Template does not exempt the *Consumer* from responsibility for the *Purpose Statement* it creates.

The *Federation Consortium* referred to in § [Considerations on the governance of the Cross-Border Federation](./05-11-00_federation-governance.md), capitalizing on the effects of the proposed experimentation, defines in detail how to implement this functionality.
