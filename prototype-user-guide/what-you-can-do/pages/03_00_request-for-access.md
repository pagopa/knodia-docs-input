The access request is the digital process performed by a **Consumer** to request the use of a **Cross-Border E-Service** published by a **Producer**.

The **Consumer**, after searching for the **Cross-Border E-Service** on the **Producer's Knode**, can initiate the access request only if they meet the **Attributes** specified in the **Consumption Requirements** defined by the **Producer**.

The access request involves two steps:
- **Authorization request**: Initiated by the **Consumer** on their own **Knode**.
- **Purpose Statement**: The **Consumer**'s declaration of the purpose of use and how the received data will be processed.

Please note that in the **Knodia Prototype**, the **Cross-Border E-Service** can be defined only in **Provide-data** mode, thereby making the **Consumer** responsible for creating the **Purpose Statement**. For **Cross-Border E-Service** mockups that implement the **Receive-data** mode, the **Purpose Statement** is created by the **Producer**.
