**Knodes** allow operators of an onboarded organization, through the **Knode Catalogue**, to consult and manage **Cross-Border E-Services**.

As a **Consumer**, you can:
- search for **Cross-Border E-Services** published by **Producers** of other **Knodes**;
- view the information sheets for **Cross-Border E-Services** of interest;
- consult and manage **Cross-Border E-Services** for which there is a request, regardless of the status of the request (e.g. verify acceptance of a **Agreement Request**).

As a **Producer**, you can:
- consult and manage defined **Cross-Border E-Services**, regardless of their status (e.g. suspend an active **Cross-Border E-Services**).
