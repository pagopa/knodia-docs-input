An **Cross-Border E-service** is a digital service that enables communication and data exchange between organizations at two different **Knodes**. Its purpose is to make data and digital functionalities of one organization (called **Producer**) securely, standardly, and controllably available to other organization (called **Consumer**).

**Cross-Border E-service** represent the core of the **Knodia** interoperability model: each organization can either produce or consume digital services from other organizations, ensuring traceability, security, and centralized permission management.
