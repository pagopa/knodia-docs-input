**Keychains** are the tool available to organizations who have completed onboarding a **Knode** to store public cryptographic material.

**Knodes** ensure access to those who need it, primarily to other **Knodes** for the identification of the organizations.


The **Knodia Prototype** manages three types of **Keychain**:
- **Consumption**, to store the public keys used by its clients to identify themselves in order to access a Producer's **Cross-Border E-service**;
- **Production**, to store the public keys used to sign the responses of its own **Cross-Border E-service**;
- **Knode API**, to store the public keys used by its clients to identify themselves in order to access the APIs made available by the **Knode** where the organization is onboarded.
